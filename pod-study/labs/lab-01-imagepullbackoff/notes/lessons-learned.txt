The ImagePullBackOff status indicates that the 
issue occurred before the app was launched.


In this case, the app logs are not the best place to start,
as the container never even started.

The best course of action for investigation is:
kubectl describe pod POD-NAME

The focus should be on the section Event

Common causes:


The image name is incorrect.
The image version does not exist.
The image is private and requires login details.
A connection issue with the image database.
