Probes Lab 01

Scenario

A web Deployment was released with readiness and liveness probes.
The manifests were accepted by Kubernetes, but the application is not becoming available correctly.

Your tasks

Apply the lab without editing it.
Inspect the Pods, events, logs, probes, and Service endpoints.
Identify every probe-related problem.
Document the symptoms, commands, observations, and root cause in investigation.md.
Commit the investigation separately.
Fix the Deployment.
Verify Pod readiness, restart behavior, rollout status, and Service endpoints.
Create solution-and-verification.md after the fix.
Commit the final solution.

Rules

Do not edit the manifests before finishing the investigation.
Do not apply the example manifests.
Keep all documentation in English.
